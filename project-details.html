Project Overview

Nexus Tech approached me to develop a complete brand identity system that would position them as innovators in the artificial intelligence and machine learning space. The challenge was to create a visual language that felt both cutting-edge and approachable, technical yet human-centered.

The project scope included comprehensive logo development, brand guidelines documentation, color palette and typography systems, icon set design, business collateral templates, and motion graphics elements for digital applications. Every touchpoint was carefully considered to ensure consistency and impact across all brand interactions.
